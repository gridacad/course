Course Curriculum

12 Immersive Sessions

Each session combines theory, engineering calculations, and practical design deliverables — fully updated to IEEE/IEC standards.

01. Introduction to Substation Primary Design
Substation functions, voltage hierarchy, major equipment overview (GIS/AIS), engineering disciplines, design stages, project lifecycle, and primary design deliverables mapping.
02. Substation Layout Design & Equipment Arrangement
Optimized equipment positioning, safety clearances, operational & maintenance access, land utilization strategies, and layout philosophies for AIS/GIS substations.
03. Auxiliary Transformer Sizing & Station Service
Determination of AC auxiliary loads, transformer kVA rating, redundancy philosophy, N+1 criteria, and step-by-step station service calculation with practical examples.
04. Substation Raceways Design – Part I (Fundamentals)
Cable trays, conduits, trenches, routing philosophy, separation requirements, segregation of control & power cables, industry best practices & installation standards.
05. Substation Raceways Design – Part II (Sizing & Calculations)
Raceway loading calculations, tray fill analysis, conduit fill per NEC/IEC, cable pulling tensions, detailed engineering design for large substations.
06. Grounding System Design & Calculations
Complete grounding grid design per IEEE 80 / IEC 50522, soil resistivity, conductor sizing, grid resistance, touch & step voltage, safety compliance verification.
07. Lightning Protection & Shielding Design
Rolling sphere method, shield wire placement, mast positioning, risk assessment (IEC 62305), shielding failures analysis & lightning protection studies.
08. Substation AC & DC Systems Design
AC distribution architecture, DC battery & charger sizing, single-line diagrams, breaker coordination, cable sizing for station service and control circuits.
09. Earthing Design for Structures & Equipment
Equipment grounding, structural bonding, buried grounding systems, corrosion protection, step/touch voltage mitigation for fences & steel structures.
10. MV System Design & Civil Interfaces
Medium voltage switchgear, cable routing through civil structures, manholes, duct banks, coordination with civil foundation drawings and construction details.
11. Integrated Substation Design Project
Full design project: layout, grounding, lightning, raceway and auxiliary systems — participants produce a complete primary design package.
12. Project Review, Assessment & Professional Feedback
Design evaluation, technical interview simulation, feedback from industry expert, certification eligibility and career guidance.
