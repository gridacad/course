Course Curriculum

8 Immersive Sessions

Each session combines theory, live ETAP demonstrations, and hands-on practice — fully aligned with IEEE/IEC industry standards.

01. Power System Fundamentals & ETAP Introduction
Power system structure, generation-to-load hierarchy, and the ETAP interface. Single-line diagram creation, manual vs. auto-build modeling, and building your first ETAP project from a real industrial network sketch.
02. Load Flow Analysis & Scenario Wizard
Steady-state load flow theory, bus types, and load modeling techniques. Configure ETAP's Scenario Wizard to compare normal, peak-load, and outage conditions, then interpret voltage profiles, loading, and losses.
03. Short Circuit Analysis & Study Automation
Fault current calculation using IEC/ANSI methods across 3-phase, line-line, and line-ground faults. Automate repetitive studies with ETAP's Scenario & Study Wizard and validate equipment withstand ratings.
04. Protection Fundamentals & Device Coordination
Protection philosophy, relay types, and coordination requirements — speed, selectivity, and sensitivity. Transformer, feeder, and motor protection schemes, with hands-on time-current curve coordination in ETAP.
05. Motor Starting Analysis
Motor starting characteristics, starting methods (DOL, star-delta, VFD), and their impact on system voltage and stability. Dynamic acceleration studies in ETAP with evaluation against IEEE 399 / IEC limits.
06. Harmonics Analysis & Reporting
Harmonic sources, distortion metrics (THD/IHD), and IEEE 519 / IEC 61000 compliance. Frequency scans, resonance identification, and passive filter design for harmonic mitigation.
07. Comprehensive Review, Q&A & Study Workshop
A full recap of load flow, short circuit, protection, motor starting, and harmonics studies. Troubleshoot common ETAP modeling mistakes and work through real industrial case discussions in an open Q&A.
08. Final Project Presentation & Professional Workflow
Present a complete ETAP project — layout, load flow, short circuit, protection coordination, motor starting, and harmonics — as professional consultants do, with personalized feedback and career guidance.
